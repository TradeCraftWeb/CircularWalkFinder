ℹ️ How it works

Routes are generated from OpenStreetMap data via the OpenRouteService round-trip directions API. Footpath percentages and repetition scores are estimates based on OSM waytype data — always verify rights of way and conditions on the ground.

⚠️ Verify on the ground. Rights of way, path conditions, and access may differ from what OSM data indicates.
